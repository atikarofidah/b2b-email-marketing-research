# Research Sources: [Email Marketing for B2B SaaS]

## LinkedIn Experts
1. [Chase Dimond](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chasedimond/)
   - **Annotation:** Expert on [email copywriting] and [campaign strategy]. Mostly posts daily tips around subject lines, CTAs, and email sequences.
2. [Stephen Provenzano](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephen-provenzano/)
   - **Annotation:** Expert on [email design] and [email development]. Regularly shares thoughts on  email frameworks, ESP tools, and scalable email templates for B2B brands.
3. [Muhammad Ahmad Azhar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/muhammad-ahmad-azhar-a1b446253/)
   - **Annotation:** An [AI Automation Engineer] and [Email Infrastructure Specialist]. He provides deep technical insights into email deliverability (DNS/DMARC) and builds complex automation workflows using n8n and AI agents to scale B2B pipelines and reduce operational debt.
4. [Daniel O Blake](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danielowenblakeaiemailmarketing/)
   - **Annotation:** A specialist in [AI-driven email marketing] and [automation]. He focuses on integrating AI into email workflows to improve personalization at scale and optimizing deliverability for B2B companies.
5. [Jose Gonsalo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josegonsalo/)
   - **Annotation:** Expert in [cold email infrastructure] and [deliverability]. He provides deep dives into technical setups like custom tracking domains, inbox rotation, and advanced lead scraping techniques to ensure high-volume outreach actually hits the primary inbox.
6. [Samar Owais](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samarowais/)
   - **Annotation:** Expert on [SaaS onboarding] and [retention emails]. Her content is very specific: why B2B SaaS welcome sequences are different from ecommerce, how to structure re-engagement emails, etc.
7. [Muhammad Abdullah](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abdullah-b2b-sales/)
   - **Annotation:** A specialist in [B2B Lead Generation] and [Outbound Sales Strategy]. He focuses on building high-quality prospect lists and crafting personalized cold outreach sequences that bypass spam filters and land directly in the primary inbox.
