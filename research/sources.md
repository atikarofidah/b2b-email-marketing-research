# Research Sources: [Email Marketing for B2B SaaS]

## LinkedIn Experts
1. [Chase Diamond](https://www.linkedin.com/in/chasedimond/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on [email copywriting] and [campaign strategy]. Mostly posts daily tips around subject lines, CTAs, and email sequences.
2. [Stephen Provenzano](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephen-provenzano/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on [email design] and [email development]. Regularly shares thoughts on  email frameworks, ESP tools, and scalable email templates for B2B brands.
3. [Muhammad Ahmad Azhar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/muhammad-ahmad-azhar-a1b446253/)
   - **Anotation:** An [AI Automation Engineer] and [Email Infrastructure Specialist]. He provides deep technical insights into email deliverability (DNS/DMARC) and builds complex automation workflows using n8n and AI agents to scale B2B pipelines and reduce operational debt.
4. [Emily Kramer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emilykramer/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on B2B SaaS [marketing strategy] and building marketing functions from scratch. Regularly shares insight soal pipeline building and campaign planning.
5. [Amanda Natividad](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amandanat/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on B2B [brand strategy] and [audience research]. Frequently posts about messaging, storytelling for SaaS, and how to write content that connects to buyer psychology.
6. [Samar Owais](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samarowais/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on [SaaS onboarding] and [retention emails]. Her content is very specific: why B2B SaaS welcome sequences are different from ecommerce, how to structure re-engagement emails, etc.
7. [Eman Ismail](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eman-i307/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on [email strategy] and [copywriting]. He frequently discusses trust-building through email, tone of voice, and what's actually changing in email marketing in 2025-2026.
8. [Val Geisler](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lovevalgeisler/)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on [lifecycle email strategy] for SaaS. One of the first to develop this niche—many email practitioners now learn from him. His focus is on onboarding sequences, churn prevention emails, and retention.

## YouTube Experts
9. [Alex Berman](https://www.youtube.com/@AlexBerman)
   - **Anotation:** Expert on [cold email outreach] for B2B. His content is highly practical: frameworks, template breakdowns, and prospecting for SaaS. One of the most frequently cited topics in B2B outbound marketing circles.
10. [Dan Martell](https://www.youtube.com/@danmartell)
   - **Anotation:** Serial SaaS founder and operator with direct B2B SaaS scaling experience.
