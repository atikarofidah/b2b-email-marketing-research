# GTM Is Often Just Cold Email

The uncomfortable truth: your GTM strategy is just cold email in disguise.

To me, for B2B SaaS, "GTM" is an alias for cold email outreach. You may not agree.

However, email is a proven, effective, cost-efficient method to market SaaS products.

It's just that so much other irrelevant noise gets pulled into it.

RevOps believe they have to do this and that... when the most important tactic is to use a consistent communication platform to deliver information to segmented users during key stages of their journey:

- Signups
- Activation
- Conversion
- Retention

This does not have to be complicated.

GTM for B2B SaaS means cold email marketing backed by a powerful strategy.
