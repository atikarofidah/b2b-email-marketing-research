# Send More Emails (The Right Way)

Bit of "basic bro" email advice today, but please, for the love of all things email: **send more emails**.

And no, that does not mean "blast your list daily."

No one is sitting in their inbox, waiting for your email.

They're not opening everything you send.

They're missing a lot, which means your "I don't want to spam them" fear is costing you sales.

If you've got something worth saying, send more emails.

## What to focus on

- Make them conversational.
- Make them direct and to the point.
- Make them human: tell a story, a joke, or a pun.

Because your list might be thousands of subscribers, but your email is always being read by one person on the other side of the screen.

Your email's job is to make a connection with that one person.
