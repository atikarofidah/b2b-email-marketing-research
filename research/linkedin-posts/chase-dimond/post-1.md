# Stop Optimizing the Wrong Email Variables

**Post date:** April 25, 2026

Most marketers are "optimizing" emails every week.

Almost none are touching the variables that actually drive revenue.

Here's what actually deserves your attention (in order of impact):

## 1) Subject lines = leverage

This is the gate.

No open -> no click -> no conversion.

- Test curiosity vs clarity
- Test short vs long
- Test pain vs outcome

Small change. Massive impact.

## 2) Discount timing (especially in abandoned carts)

When you show the offer matters more than the offer itself.

- Too early = lost margin
- Too late = lost sale

Find the sweet spot.

## 3) Number of emails in your flows

Most brands under-send.

- Add one more email
- Then another
- Until performance drops

You'll be surprised how far you can push it.

## 4) Offer type

Not all discounts convert equally.

- % off vs $ off vs free shipping

The "winner" depends on your product and AOV.

Test it. Don't guess.

## 5) Plain text vs designed emails

This one hurts designers, but...

- Plain text often wins in flows

Because it feels like a real message, not a campaign.

## 6) Preview text (the most ignored lever)

Almost nobody optimizes this.

- It's literally your second subject line

And most brands leave it to chance.

## The truth

- Better emails don't come from creativity alone
- They come from prioritizing what actually moves metrics

Test smarter -> not more.
