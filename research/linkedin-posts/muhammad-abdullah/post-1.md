# The 7-Touch SMB Cold Email System

Small business owners are the hardest to reach.

Until you know this 7-step cold email system.

I've run cold email campaigns targeting SMB owners for years. Here's what actually moves the needle, broken down touch by touch.

## The 7-touch sequence that books SMB meetings

### Day 1 - The hook

Lead with their pain.

"Most [industry] owners I talk to are struggling with [X]."

Short. Personal. No pitch yet.

### Day 3 - The proof

Drop a micro case study. One result. One sentence.

"We helped a company like yours add 12 new clients in 60 days."

### Day 5 - The value add

Give something free:

- A tip
- A resource
- A quick audit offer

Build trust before asking for time.

### Day 8 - The soft ask

"Would it make sense to connect for 15 mins?"

Low commitment. Easy yes. No long calendar links.

### Day 12 - The reframe

Come at it differently:

- New subject line
- New angle

"Thought about you when I saw this..."

Re-ignite curiosity.

### Day 16 - The social proof

Name-drop a similar SMB you've helped. Specificity sells.

"A 12-person logistics firm in your area saw X result."

### Day 21 - The breakup

"I won't follow up after this - but the door's always open."

Breakup emails get the highest reply rates. Every time.

## Stack

Apollo to find SMB leads -> SendGrid to deliver -> HubSpot to track every touch automatically.

## Performance insight

Most replies land between touch 3 and 5.

Never stop at 2.

## Practical rule

Personalize the first line of every email.

Everything else can be templated.
