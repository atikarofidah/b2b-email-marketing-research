## Why This Still Matters

- 77% of B2B buyers prefer email over any other channel
- 81% of B2B marketers say email is their most-used content distribution channel

The channel is not broken. The execution usually is.

## Step 1: Build a Foundation Before You Send

- Create lead magnets: eBooks, checklists, ROI calculators (value in exchange for email)
- Send educational newsletters: position as advisor, not salesperson
- Launch an onboarding sequence: welcome -> setup -> key features -> support

## Step 2: Stop Sending the Same Email to Everyone

- Segment by job role, industry, product usage, and buying stage
- Send technical tips to IT teams
- Send ROI case studies to executives

Additional benchmarks:

- Emails from named individuals get 27% higher open rates than generic company names
- Segmented campaigns can drive 30% more opens and 50% more click-throughs (HubSpot 2026)

One list, many conversations. That's segmentation.

## Step 3: Move Engaged Subscribers Toward a Decision

- Host live webinars and product walkthroughs to create urgency through access
- Offer time-limited trials or discounts (example: "Get 3 months free if you upgrade before Friday")
- Use ROI-based proof with personalized data on time or money saved

Prospects at this stage do not need more information.
They need a reason to act now.

## Step 4: Stop Optimizing for Open Rates Alone

Open rates are now unreliable (Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates numbers).

Track these instead:

- CTR: 3-5% benchmark for B2B
- Conversion rate: sign-ups, demos, purchases
- Unsubscribe rate: keep below 0.5%
- Spam complaint rate: keep below 0.1% (Gmail/Yahoo will filter or block above this)

Clicks and conversions tell you what opens cannot.

## Step 5: Craft the Email Itself

- Subject line: specific, under 7 words, outcome-led
- Body: short sentences, clear value, no jargon
- CTA: one action only ("Start Free Trial", "Book a Demo", "Download Now")
- Design: mobile-first (65% of emails are opened on mobile in 2026)
- Sender: never use a generic company sender name; use a real person's name

If it takes more than 30 seconds to read, it's too long.

## Step 6: Let Automation Do the Heavy Lifting

- Trigger emails based on user actions: sign-ups, feature use, inactivity
- Build drip sequences that educate over time, not just follow-ups
- Use AI tools for content personalization at scale (58% of B2B email teams already do)

Automated emails drive 320% more revenue at 2% of total send volume.

Automation is not replacing personalization.
It's making it possible at scale.
